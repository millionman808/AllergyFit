Common questions

Where do the nutrition numbers come from?

Every calorie and macro comes from the USDA FoodData Central database. The AI only identifies which foods are on your plate and their portion sizes — it never invents the numbers. That is the core difference between SafeFuel and other AI calorie apps.

How does the allergen check work?

You set your triggers and how severely you react to each, from mild to anaphylaxis. Every meal, product, and recipe is then checked against that list, and anything containing a trigger is flagged. The check is deliberately cautious — it would rather flag something than miss it.

It is still not a substitute for reading the label. Product recipes change and food databases lag behind.

Can I use it without an account?

Yes — tap View demo on the sign-in screen to explore the app with sample data.

How do I delete my account and data?

Email eliszip@gmail.com from your account address and we will delete everything within 30 days.

Managing your subscription

Subscriptions are billed through Apple. To change or cancel, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Something looks wrong in a nutrition result

Tap an ingredient in the result to correct its portion — the macros recalculate from the database values. If a food is matched to the wrong item entirely, email us the meal description and we will look into it.
